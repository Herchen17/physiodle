How Physiodle works

Short answers to the questions players ask most.
How does it work?

One case a day. Five clues, revealed one at a time. Each wrong guess reveals the next clue. Five wrong and the answer is shown, with a clinical insight explaining the case.

How is it scored?

Solve on the first clue for 5 points, the second for 4, down to 1 for the fifth. A miss scores 0. Only puzzles solved on the day they come out count for points and the leaderboard.

What counts as a streak?

Consecutive days solved on the day the puzzle came out. Playing the archive doesn't extend or break a streak. Your streak shows next to the date at the top once you're signed in, and it celebrates at 3, 7, 14, 30, 50, 100 and 365 days.

When does the new puzzle come out?

Midnight in your own timezone. Everyone gets the same puzzle number on the same calendar date, wherever they are.

Can I play more than one a day?

Yes. Sign in and open Archive in the top bar to play any past puzzle. Archive games count towards your played and won totals, but not towards points or streaks.

My answer was right but wasn't accepted.

Spelling variants, abbreviations and many synonyms are accepted. If a genuine equivalent was rejected, tap Something wrong with this puzzle? under the answer, choose "My guess should have counted" and pick the guess. Every report is read and matching is fixed from them.

The answer wasn't a diagnosis.

Some cases are post-surgical or management scenarios. The line above the guess box tells you which kind of answer is expected: a diagnosis, a procedure the patient is recovering from, a clinical scenario, or a movement pattern. These are being moved to true diagnoses over time.

Why doesn't the dropdown show every condition?

The suggestions list holds thousands of real conditions, not just the answers, so scrolling it won't give anything away. Type a few letters of what you mean and pick from the list, or type the full name.

How do I put Physiodle on my phone?

Tap Get app in the top bar for step-by-step instructions for iPhone, Android and computer. No app store needed. It opens full screen, remembers your login and keeps your streak one tap away.

How do daily reminders work?

One notification a day at the time you choose, and nothing else. Turn them on from the Get app screen. On iPhone, add Physiodle to your home screen first, then open it from there to turn reminders on.

What's the share link on my profile?

Your share text and share card carry your personal link. Anyone who signs up through it counts on your profile, and you level up as friends join.

Does my account work on Pharmodle?

Yes. The same email and password sign you in to both games.

I forgot my password.

Tap Sign In, then Forgot your password?. A reset link goes to the email on your account and works for 30 minutes. Confirm your email from your profile so this always works.

Is this clinical advice?

No. Physiodle is a study game. Cases are simplified and may contain errors. See the Terms of Use.
